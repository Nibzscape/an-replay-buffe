# Privacy Policy for AN Replay Buffer

**Last updated: 5 October 2026**

AN Byteworks ("we", "us", or "our") operates the AN Replay Buffer Android application ("the App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use AN Replay Buffer.

## 1. Screen Recordings and Media

AN Replay Buffer allows users to record their device screen and, when selected by the user, include audio and/or camera footage.

Screen recordings, audio recordings, camera footage, and saved replay clips are created at the user's request and are stored on the user's device.

AN Byteworks does not operate a server for uploading or storing your screen recordings, microphone recordings, camera footage, or saved replay clips.

The App requires Android permissions and system authorisation where necessary to provide these recording features.

## 2. Camera and Microphone

AN Replay Buffer may request access to your device's camera and microphone.

Camera access is used for features such as Face Cam when enabled by the user.

Microphone access may be used to include microphone audio in recordings when selected by the user.

Camera and microphone data used for recordings is processed as part of the recording functionality. AN Byteworks does not operate a server that receives or stores this recorded camera or microphone content.

You can manage these permissions through your Android device settings.

## 3. Advertising

The free version of AN Replay Buffer may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes including providing, measuring, securing, and, where permitted, personalising advertisements.

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or provide privacy choices relating to advertising.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

AN Replay Buffer uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes, errors, and stability problems.

Crashlytics may automatically collect diagnostic information such as crash logs, device and operating system information, app version information, device identifiers or installation identifiers, and technical information associated with a crash.

This information is used to diagnose problems, improve reliability, and maintain the App.

## 5. In-App Purchases

AN Replay Buffer offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade through Google Play.

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. AN Byteworks does not directly collect or store your full payment card or banking details.

The App may receive information from Google Play about the status of a purchase so that it can determine whether Pro features should be enabled.

Google Play's handling of purchase and payment information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

## 6. Information We Collect

AN Byteworks does not require users to create an AN Byteworks account to use the App.

We do not intentionally collect the contents of your screen recordings, camera recordings, microphone recordings, or saved replay clips on AN Byteworks servers.

Third-party services integrated into the App, including Google AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics, may automatically process certain device, advertising, usage, and diagnostic information as described in this Privacy Policy and their own documentation.

## 7. How Information Is Used

Information processed through third-party services may be used to:

- provide and operate advertising;
- obtain and respect advertising consent choices;
- diagnose crashes and technical problems;
- improve App stability and performance;
- prevent fraud and abuse; and
- verify Google Play purchases and enable purchased features.

## 8. Data Sharing

AN Byteworks does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate features of the App, particularly Google services including AdMob, Firebase Crashlytics, the User Messaging Platform, and Google Play Billing.

These providers process information according to their respective privacy policies, terms, and applicable law.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Recordings saved by AN Replay Buffer remain under the user's control on their device and can be deleted by the user.

Information processed by Google services may be retained according to Google's applicable retention policies.

If you contact AN Byteworks directly by email, we may retain the correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain support records, or meet legal obligations.

## 10. Children's Privacy

AN Replay Buffer is not specifically designed or directed at children.

Advertising and other third-party services may be subject to additional restrictions depending on the user's region, age, consent status, and applicable law.

## 11. Security

We take reasonable steps when designing and maintaining AN Replay Buffer to protect user privacy and limit access to information to what is necessary for the App's functionality.

However, no electronic system or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
